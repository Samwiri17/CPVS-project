About Us

Who We Are

CHILD PROTECTION AND VULNERABLE SUPPORT

Unicare is a non-profit organization committed to improving the lives of vulnerable individuals and communities through humanitarian support, community development, education, healthcare, food assistance, and access to clean water. We believe that everyone deserves an opportunity to live a healthy, dignified, and meaningful life.

Through community participation, partnerships, and dedicated volunteers, we work together to create sustainable solutions that empower individuals, strengthen communities, and transform lives. Our commitment is to reach those in need and provide support that creates lasting positive change.

Our Direction

Our Vision and Mission

Our vision and mission guide our commitment to humanitarian service, community empowerment, and sustainable development.

Our Vision

A south sudan where every child and vulnerable person lives in safety, dignity, and has equal access to opportunities for growth and development.

Our Mission

To protect, empower and support vulnerable children, families and communities through sustainable humanitarian and development interventions that promote safety, resilience, education, health and social inclusions.

Our Goal

To improve the well-being, protection, resilience and social-economic development of vulnerable polpulations throughout South Sudan.

What Guides Us

Our Core Values

Our work is guided by values that define our commitment to serving humanity and building stronger communities.

Integrity

Our Purpose in Action

Our Objectives

To translate our mission and vision into measurable actions, Unicare focuses on the following objectives.

1. Child Protection

1. Prevent child abuse,neglect,exploitation and violence.
2. Strengthen community-based child protection systems.
3. Promote children's rights and welfare.

2. Education Support

1. Increase access to quality education for vulnerable children.
2. Support school enrollment and retention.
3. Promote safe learning environment.

3. Health and Nutrition

1. Improve access to essential health services.
2. Support maternal and child health programs.
3. Reduce malnutrition among vulnerable children.

4. Gender-based Violence Prevention.

1. Provide support services for survivors.
2. Increase awardness of GBV prevention.
3. Strengthen community protection mechanisms.

5. Livelihoods and Economic Development

1. Strengthen household resilience.
2. Promote vocational skills development.
3. Support income-generating activities.

6. Emergency Response

1. Deliver rapid humanitarian assistance during emergencies.
2. Strengthen diaster preparedness and response mechanisms.

7. Mental Health and Psychosocial Support

1. Provide counseling and psychosocial services.
2. Support trauma recovery among affected populations.

8. Disability Inclusion

1. Promote the rights and inclusion of persons living with disabilies.
2. Improve access to services for vulnerable groups.

Welcome to CPVS

We are here to help everyone in need

Child Protection and Vulnerable Support (CPVS) is here for children, women and families facing poverty, conflicts and displacement in South Sudan.

We provide protection, education, health food, clean water, and emergency support so that every child can live safe and hopefull.

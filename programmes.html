Our Programmes

What We Do

Program Areas

CPVS implements integrated programs that protect children, strengthen families and build resilient communities across South Sudan.

Program 01

Child Protection Program

Protecting children from harm, abuse and exploitation.

  • Case management
  • Family tracing and reunification
  • Child-friendly spaces
  • Child rights awareness
  • Protection monitoring
  • Prevention of child labor
Program 02

Education Program

Ensuring every child has access to quality learning.

  • School support
  • Teacher training
  • Distribution of learning materials
  • School rehabilitation
  • Scholarship support
Program 03

Health and Nutrition Program

Promoting healthy children and healthy communities.

  • Health awareness campaigns
  • Nutrition screening
  • Maternal and child healthcare
  • Growth monitoring
  • Community health promotion
Program 04

Gender and Protection Program

Promoting equality, safety and dignity for all.

  • GBV prevention
  • Women's empowerment initiatives
  • Protection referrals
  • Community sensitization
Program 05

Livelihood and Economic Recovery Program

Building skills and income for families and youth.

  • Skills training
  • Business development support
  • Village Savings and Loans Associations (VSLA)
  • Youth employment initiatives
Program 06

WASH Program

Clean water, sanitation and hygiene for all.

  • Clean water access
  • Hygiene promotion
  • Sanitation facilities
  • Community-led total sanitation
Program 07

Emergency Response Program

Rapid life-saving support in times of crisis.

  • Emergency food assistance
  • Shelter support
  • NFI distribution
  • Rapid needs assessments
